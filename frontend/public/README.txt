Drop your background brain image here as `brain-bg.jpg` (or `.png`).

It will be rendered fullscreen behind the rotating 3D brain. The image
is automatically:
  - scaled to cover the viewport
  - blurred slightly
  - tinted (hue-rotated) to match the cyan/violet palette
  - blended with `mix-blend-screen` so it glows instead of feeling pasted on

If no image is present, the page falls back to just the 3D brain — no error.

Recommended:
  - Dark, high-contrast brain photo or render
  - Resolution: at least 1920x1080
  - Format: jpg or webp for smaller file size

You can find free options at unsplash.com (search "brain") or pexels.com.
